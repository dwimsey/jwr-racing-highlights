September 18

The bounce-back

Qualified P3 and turned sub-15-second laps, then a lap-11 spin and contact. Everyone walked away. Thank you to Andrew Tyree, Charlie Hansen, and all the track staff and EMS who were there instantly to check on the kids.

Rebuild

Friends put it back together

The track family started figuring out the repair that same night — thank you Noah “Buddy” Haislip, Chad Williams, Heather and Tony Rodriquez, and the Chriscoe crew. The car was back together for championship night.

October 9

Championship night

Qualified 3rd, started 3rd, and finished 6th in the Capital 170 championship feature.
